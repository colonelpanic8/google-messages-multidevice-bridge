Google Messages Bridge

Pair this browser securely

This helper keeps setup in its own tab. It reads the seven allowlisted Google cookies only after you click Connect, sends them directly to your chosen bridge with a one-time ticket, and then releases site access.

Setup

  1. Enter the bridge origin and one-time pairing ticket displayed by your bridge.
  2. Open Google sign-in and finish signing in to the account used by Google Messages.
  3. Return to this tab and explicitly connect the account.

HTTPS is required except for loopback and literal Tailscale 100.64.0.0/10 addresses.

This is not your API bearer token. The bridge accepts it once and expires it after ten minutes.

Cookie access is narrowly scoped. Required: SID, HSID, OSID, SSID, APISID, SAPISID. Optional: __Secure-1PSIDTS. Nothing is saved by this extension.
