Connect your phone

Start reconnection here, then confirm the emoji in Google Messages on your phone. If background recovery is enrolled, no browser setup is needed.

Reconnecting cancels queued sends. Your saved messages remain available.

With a different phone, earlier conversations stay read-only until the new phone shows them.

Use browser sign-in

Use this if your saved sign-in no longer works, or to connect a different Google account.

First time? Install the Chrome helper

Download the pairing helper, extract it, then open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked.

  1. Choose Start browser sign-in below.
  2. Open the installed helper from Chrome’s extensions menu and copy the bridge URL and ticket into its setup tab.
  3. Choose Connect this Google account. If needed, use the helper’s sign-in button first.
  4. Return here and confirm the emoji on your phone.
